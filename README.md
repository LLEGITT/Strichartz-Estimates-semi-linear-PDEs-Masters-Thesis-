## 🔬 Research Overview & Core Topics

This work explores the intersection of harmonic analysis and dispersive partial differential equations (PDEs). The core focus is establishing low-regularity bounds and analyzing the qualitative behavior of solutions to the **Semi-Linear Schrödinger Equation (NLS)** with a cubic non-linearity ($SNL_3$).

Because dispersive equations cause different frequencies to propagate at different speeds, waves undergo spatial smoothing over time. This project handles the rigorous analytical quantification of this phenomenon under restrictive, low-regularity constraints.

### Key Mathematical Frameworks Explored:
*   **Strichartz Estimates & $TT^*$ Method:** Utilized duality arguments and interpolation to derive sharp space-time integrability estimates for linear and semi-linear flows.
*   **Littlewood-Paley Theory:** Leveraged dyadic decompositions to isolate frequency bands, allowing for precise control of solution bounds in Sobolev and Besov spaces.
*   **Atomic Decomposition:** Evaluated structural decompositions within specific solution spaces to analyze critical energy boundaries and potential singularity formations (wave focusing/blow-up behavior).
